# BOM notes

- Rows 1 to 8 are numbered to match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the component table in the design precis (TCT-PRC-001).
- All prices are indicative USD for one-off prototype quantities, as of September 2026, and will be replaced with quoted prices at TRL 3.
- Rows 1 to 6 and 8 make one cartridge: about $49. Row 7 (adapter frame) is per host slot: about $15.
- One cartridge plus one frame is about $64, within the $80 prototype budget. A set of one cartridge in each of the three proposed grades plus one frame would be about $162; that would need a budget change, proposed, awaiting Amish.
- PCM prices vary widely with quantity. The $10 per cartridge assumes about 1.1 kg at sample-quantity prices; bulk paraffin grades are cheaper.
