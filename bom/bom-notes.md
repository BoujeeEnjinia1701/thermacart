# BOM notes

- Rows 1 to 8 are numbered to match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the component table in the design precis (TCT-PRC-001 v0.4). Quantities and sizes follow the parametric model `cad/src/model.py`.
- All prices are indicative USD for one-off prototype quantities, as of September 2026. They are estimates, not quotes, and must be replaced with supplier quotes before any build.
- Rows 1 to 6 and 8 make one cartridge: $80.00, including about $5 of primer and matte black high-temperature paint for the finish decided in TCT-DDR-002 (O8) and the parts added to make the design constructable (TCT-DDR-003, 2026-10-01: bonded seals, bolts, phenolic washers, a G 1/2 plug, rivets). Row 7 (adapter frame) is per host slot: $17.00, including four rivets. `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` reads this file and prints these totals (TCT-CAL-001, section H).
- Value engineering: `budget_usd` in `project.yaml` is a hypothetical value-engineering target, not a limit (Amish, 2026-10-01). Value-engineering target: USD 80 for one C5 cartridge and one frame (TCT-DDR-001, D6). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 97 (USD 17 over the target). Cost drivers and savings worth trying are in the design decisions register (`docs/06-design-decisions.md`). Supplier quotes (TCT-DDR-002, O6) wait with TRL 4, which is on hold.
- Decided by Amish on 2026-10-02 (TCT-DEC-001, items 7 to 9), not yet written into the BOM lines: an 18 mm painted grade band round the nose end as a masking step in row 1; the painted side marking "ThermaCart, TC-L C5" and a printed conditioning time on the C5 label in the label set (row 6); a "C5 ONLY" decal on the frame (row 7). Rubber feet are left to each host.
- A set of one cartridge in each of the three grades plus one frame would be about $258.
- Row 2 is priced for the C5 grade at $10/kg. The C25 and H70 fills are 1.12 and 1.18 kg. PCM prices vary widely with quantity; at $20/kg a cartridge costs about $11 more.
- The caps (row 3) are built from flat 6061 plate cut and filed, so no milling is needed. The frame (row 7) needs a hand brake, for example at a makerspace.
